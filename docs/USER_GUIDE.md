# Using MailAutomator

1. **Connect your sender.** In Settings → Provider, save your Resend key and webhook secret. In Senders, add your address and check domain verification.
2. **Import a spreadsheet.** Choose CSV, XLS or XLSX (up to 10 MB). The processor scans every sheet in a browser worker, tracks original cell coordinates, normalizes addresses, rejects malformed entries, and deduplicates across the workbook. Confidence above 50% identifies likely email columns; emails outside those columns are still found. Review valid and invalid entries, remove unwanted recipients, then import. Suppressed contacts remain suppressed on reimport.
3. **Organize contacts.** Search, sort, filter, and paginate the contacts table. Select contacts to export CSV, change status, delete, or add to a group. Create groups under Settings. Contact details show import and campaign history. Only contacts marked active are eligible for sending.
4. **Write a campaign.** Name it, select contacts from an import/group or individually, choose a verified sender, and compose. Load a template or save your own. Add attachments within the 10 MB combined limit. Review the sandboxed preview, save a draft, send a test to your account, schedule in your browser's local time, or confirm Send Now.
5. **Track delivery.** A queued campaign starts with the next worker invocation. Reports show per-recipient state and errors; provider acceptance differs from confirmed delivery. Complaints unsubscribe the contact, and bounces suppress it. Analytics measures acceptance success among finished attempts.
6. **Build workflows.** Add email, delay, and status-condition steps. Reorder with arrow buttons. Drafts can be edited; active/paused workflows have fixed steps so enrollments remain consistent. Each contact enrolls once per workflow. A scheduled trigger enrolls new eligible contacts at each occurrence, using the selected timezone. Waits are minimum delays, and actions execute on worker ticks. A contact that becomes inactive exits before the next step.

Campaign deletion removes its delivery report. Contact deletion keeps queue history with the original recipient address but removes the contact relationship. Group deletion preserves contacts. Pausing does not recall messages already in flight.
